In the 'Grids' project, I visually explored modern art, covering movements from Impressionism to Pop Art. Through a series of images and descriptions, I highlighted how various artistic movements broke with classical conventions, offering new perspectives and techniques. This work serves as a visual and conceptual introduction to the main currents of modern art, emphasizing the evolution and diversity of styles that define this period."

BY: Samuel Jaramillo
