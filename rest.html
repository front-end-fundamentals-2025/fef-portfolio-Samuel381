PROJECTS

"The 'Graphics and Animation' project focuses on promoting a Mexican food festival. I designed an informative website detailing the event's activities, including painting workshops, children's theater, papier-mâché creation, puppetry, gardening and composting workshops, and a costume contest. I used vibrant graphic elements and subtle animations to reflect the festival's cultural richness and energetic atmosphere, ensuring the information is engaging and accessible to visitors."

BY: Samuel Jaramillo
