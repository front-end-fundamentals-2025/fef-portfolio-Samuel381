PROJECTS

Acetato" is a project I developed in collaboration with Manuela and Joel. It is an introspective exploration of death and its role in shaping meaning in life. Through a visual and textual narrative, we examine how awareness of mortality drives us to create, remember, and reinterpret our experiences.
The website combines quotes from authors like Patti Smith, Mo Yan, and Seneca with our own reflections, highlighting how art can transform suffering and finitude into something meaningful. Additionally, we chose acetate as a metaphor for the fragility and transparency of existence. With this project, we aim to spark a conversation about death—not from a place of fear, but through creativity and reflection.
